# Case Study

PlanShift demonstrates product thinking around SaaS monetization, packaging, segmentation, and migration planning.

The MVP focuses on high-value PM questions: affected customers, revenue uplift, feature loss, churn risk, contract restrictions, grandfathering candidates, migration strategy, and communication planning.

The demo scenario moves SSO and Advanced Audit Logs from Pro to Enterprise, exposing realistic trade-offs between monetization and customer trust.

Resume positioning: Designed and built PlanShift, a SaaS pricing-change simulation product that helps Product Managers compare revenue impact, customer disruption, churn risk, and migration strategies before launching packaging changes.
